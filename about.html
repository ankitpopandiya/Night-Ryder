About Us 🦸

Night-Ryder is a student-focused service designed to solve late-night hostel problems like food, medicines, and urgent needs.

Our mission is to make student life easier during night hours.

How It Works

  2. We connect to vendors
  3. Fast Delivery
  4. Easy Payment
